    Generation 1: Data¶

    Generation 1 is the FY22–FY25 beam data event-built with EBV2 by Yue Feng and Steven Doran, using fengyvoid/ToolAnalysis v1.1 run through autoANNIE. It is the data behind the CC-inclusive, CC0π, MicroBooNE–ANNIE CC0π joint, NCQE and first LAPPD (JINST 21 P02002) analyses, and the theses of Steven Doran (DocDB 6712), James Minock (DocDB 6976) and Yue Feng (DocDB 6705).

    It covers FY22, FY23 (including SANDI I), FY24 runs 4725–4809 (MRD stable), and FY25 (including SANDI II): about 8.3e20 POT. See Run periods and POT.

    Blinded data

    The full Generation 1 ProcessedData and BeamClusterTrees are blinded. Please contact the analysis coordinators, Jingbo Wang and Andy Mastbaum, before accessing them, and follow the blinding scheme.

    SANDI runs

    SANDI I (runs 4208–4314; JINST 19 P05070) and SANDI II (runs 5081–5328) had a WbLS target in the tank. Do not use them unless you are doing a WbLS analysis.

    ProcessedData¶

    Recommended

    Fully event-built data, one directory of processed part files per run.

    Stage Software Path
    ProcessedData EBV2, fengyvoid/ToolAnalysis v1.1 /pnfs/annie/persistent/processed/processingData_EBV2/processed_EBV2/<run>/

    POT: about 8.3e20 (stored per event in the BoostStore) · Contact: Steven Doran

    2% unblinded subset¶

    Recommended for development

    Lets analyzers work on real data without risk of unblinding: to check MC–data compatibility of a selection as it matures, and for calibration and tuning. It is stage 3 of the blinding scheme. ProcessedData only.

    About 2% of the POT of each period, chosen at random by POT per run and keeping whole runs together.

    Stage Software Path
    ProcessedData EBV2, fengyvoid/ToolAnalysis v1.1 /pnfs/annie/persistent/processed/ANNIE_2_percent_unblinded_data/GEN1/<period>/<run>/
    Period directory Dates POT in subset POT of full period ≈ triggers in subset
    FY22/ Jan–Jul 2022 4.08e18 2.04e20 1.0e6
    FY23/SANDI_I/ Mar–May 2023 1.02e18 0.51e20 2.4e5
    FY23/FY23_REST/ Dec 2022–Jul 2023 3.50e18 1.75e20 8.4e5
    FY24/MRD_STABLE/ Feb–May 2024 0.98e18 0.49e20 2.4e5
    FY25/SANDI_II/ Nov 2024–Feb 2025 1.75e18 0.88e20 4.2e5
    FY25/FY25_REST/ Feb–Jul 2025 5.30e18 2.65e20 1.3e6

    The trigger counts are estimates from the FY22–FY23 rate (2.4e5 per 1e18 POT). POT is TOR860.

    Contact: Steven Doran
