    Getting started¶

    Before you analyse data¶

    The reference is the technote Preparing Data for Analysis (S. Doran, DocDB 6699). In short:

    1. Start from an existing processed sample. Re-process raw data only if your analysis needs a different event building or reconstruction.
    2. Follow the blinding scheme. Develop on MC first. The 2% unblinded subset is the first look at data in the signal region.
    3. Select good-beam triggers: the undelayed beam trigger, horn current, toroid range and agreement, and a paired PMT timestamp. Where needed, also require bunch rotation off, a good BRF fit, and the extended-readout mode you want. The cut values are in the technote.
    4. Compute POT from the triggers you selected, with one toroid (TOR860 is the usual choice). The run-period POT is for planning only.
    5. MC and data use different timing references. Do not copy an absolute hitT cut from MC to data.
    6. Off-beam triggers have no MRD or FMV data. If your selection needs them, use the pre-beam window to estimate beam-uncorrelated backgrounds.

    Blinding¶

    ANNIE analyses look at data in stages. The scheme written for the first νμ CC analysis (J. Minock and A. Mastbaum, Blindness Scheme for ANNIE CC Analysis, DocDB 6259) is the model:

    1. MC only. Develop the whole analysis on the GENIE central value: signal definition, selection, observables and binning, POT normalisation, systematics, unfolding.
    2. Data sidebands. Check the analysis on control regions outside the signal region, filtered from the full data.
    3. 2% of the data. The first look at the signal region, using the 2% unblinded subset. This is the last chance to change the analysis.
    4. 10% of the data. A final check before full unblinding.
    5. Full data.

    The results of each stage are shown to the collaboration before moving to the next. Analyses with neutrons, other final states or other detector configurations may need their own version of the scheme.

    Analysis coordinators: Jingbo Wang (jingbo.wang@sdsmt.edu) and Andy Mastbaum (mastbaum@physics.rutgers.edu). Contact them before accessing blinded data.

    Glossary¶

    ProcessedData
    Event-built data: ToolAnalysis BoostStores with the PMT, MRD, LAPPD, trigger and beam information of each event, one directory of part files per run. Beam information (POT per trigger) is stored with each event.
    BeamClusterTrees (BeamCluster ntuples)
    ROOT ntuples made from ProcessedData by the BeamClusterAnalysis toolchain (ANNIEEvent tree Event): event, trigger, beam, PMT-cluster and MRD information.
    EBV2
    Event building version 2 (Yue Feng, DocDB 6043).
    Generation (Gen 1, Gen 2)
    One consistent processing of the data, and the MC meant to be compared with it.
    ghep, gst
    GENIE output: the full event record (ghep) and the flat summary tree (gst).
    anniedirt (g4dirt)
    Geant4 propagation of the GENIE final state through the rock and hall to the detector.
    WCSim pmt and lappd files
    WCSim writes the PMT and LAPPD responses to separate files.
    CC tuning, NC tuning
    The two WCSim detector-response tunings. See Gen 1 MC.
    SANDI
    A water-based liquid scintillator (WbLS) target in the tank, deployed in SANDI I (Mar–May 2023; see JINST 19 P05070) and SANDI II (Nov 2024–Feb 2025). Do not use those runs unless you are doing a WbLS analysis.
    TOR860, TOR875
    The two BNB toroids that measure protons on target (POT). They agree to within a few percent.

    Status labels¶

    Label Meaning
    Recommended The default choice for its purpose.
    Available Valid and documented, but not the default.
    Analysis-specific Made for one analysis, often in a personal area. Check with the contact before relying on it.
    In production Being produced. Paths and contents may change.
    Planned Not produced yet.
    DQ only Data-quality processing. Not for final analyses.
    Deprecated Superseded and kept for reproducibility. Listed on Deprecated.
